## Hebrew surface-form ranks

Ranks and counts are derived from the Hebrew FrequencyWords/OpenSubtitles 2018 list. The isolated token `ג` at source rank 34 is excluded as an abbreviation/subtitle artifact, leaving the 100 most frequent lexical word forms. FrequencyWords documents CC BY-SA 4.0 for generated frequency content.

Source: `https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/master/content/2018/he/he_50k.txt`

Pointing, transliteration, sense separation, glosses, data structure, prompts, and visual assets in this repository are newly authored for Aleph Bet and are marked for native-speaker editorial review.
